Add a test: `partPercent(kScore(0.978))` is `"99.7"`, `partPercent(1)` is `"100"`, and the walk inputs still score 60.

The words "pebble helium drifts" in the earlier walk order were loose. Do not animate the stipple. Do not change the on-screen label.
