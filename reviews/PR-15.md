## Findings

1. Non-blocking, and it still needs the owner's yes before anyone edits `STYLE-STANDARD.md`. BWR is drawn at 270–300 °C, not 200–330. MSR is drawn at 620–680 °C, not 625–670. I checked the model. At 200–330 the BWR hold, about 275 °C cold and 283–289 °C hot, is one colour, so the drill would not show a difference. The low-flow corner is about 307 °C, which is the next band on 200–330, not the same band. The comment that says the whole range is one band is a little too strong. On 270–300 that corner clamps to the top band, and the label can still print a number above 300. MSR cold is 620 and the full-power hot leg is 678, so both sit outside 625–670. The wider scale matches the numbers the labels print. A low-flow MSR hot leg can still pass 680. None of this changes `thermalState`.
2. Non-blocking. Jet pumps, the TRISO inset, and the MSR off-gas line are not drawn. The PR says so. The step did not require them.
3. Non-blocking. The BWR layout points include 200 °C and 330 °C, which are outside the printed scale. The colour check only asserts that a clamped band is one of the seven colours, so those points do not prove the scale.

## Verdict reason

The four drawings are the same pixel system as the PWR, the two PWR facts are on the picture, and the layout tests pass. The scale change is a display choice that matches the model. It is not a frozen number. Leave `STYLE-STANDARD.md` alone until the owner confirms the two new ranges.
