## Findings

1. **Ruling 1 is in.** `partPercent` returns `"100"` only when `value === 1`. Otherwise it truncates toward zero to one decimal. `kScore(0.978)` is 0.997959 and prints `99.7`. The value column uses it (`ReactorApps.tsx` 176). The weight column is still `Math.round(p.weight * 100)`. The `1e-9` inside `Math.trunc` is a float guard, not a second rounding rule. It does not turn 0.997959 into 100.
2. **Ruling 4 is in.** The heading is the words "What to look at" at `fontSize: 12` (`ReactorApps.tsx` 181). The next line is the existing `lookLine`. No new sentence.
3. **Zero stays `0.0`.** That is the truncation rule for a part that is not full. `50` prints `50.0` and `0` prints `0.0`. Exact full stays the bare string `100`, so a full part and a tenth-short part cannot look the same. Do not special-case zero. Do not open a follow-up for it.
