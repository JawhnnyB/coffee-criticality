## How the floor's culture works

Two things your choices move, worked out fresh from the week so far (nothing new is saved):

- **Trust:** sitting with people, peer checks, backing someone up, giving a gift. Each catch adds 1.
- **Calling each other out:** reports (2 each), catches, naming a problem, telling someone they're wrong.
- **Hiding** costs 2 of each.

Five cultures:

- **Guarded:** Nobody's sure of each other yet. People are polite and careful.
- **Respect:** People call each other out because they trust each other. It lands, and nobody holds a grudge.
- **Comfort:** Everyone's kind. Nobody says the hard thing. It feels good, and that's the danger.
- **Distrust:** People say what's wrong, but as blame. Everyone keeps receipts.
- **Fear:** Heads down. Things go unsaid, and people go quiet when someone walks in.

Thresholds: Respect at trust 4 and calling-out 4; Comfort at trust 4 with calling-out 1 or less; Distrust at calling-out 4 with trust 1 or less; Fear at two hides, or either below −2; otherwise Guarded. Every week starts Guarded. Comfort is the one you didn't name: trust without calling out is its own hazard on a plant floor, so I added it.

## What you walk in on

Your 2nd and 3rd talk with each person each week opens with them and someone else. The same situation is written five ways, one per culture. It ends with them turning to you: back someone up (trust +1, and +1 trust with that person) or name the problem (calling out +1, culture +1). Afterward, the crew page shows what one of them said about the other.
