# Font audit 2026-11-14

Font audit: 0 text violations, 0 on-screen colour pairs under 4.5:1, 6 listed palette pairs under 4.5:1, across 5 viewports.

The walk is the title, the new-shift setup, the desktop, and the News window. It is not the cafe, the control room, or dialogue. The wide title has no DOM text: Begin, Continue, and Settings are baked into `menu.png` and hit by transparent targets. At 390 the plate is 1×, so those words are DOM buttons in Pixelify Sans at 16px and at least 44px tall. `IntroScreens` `hit()` uses `fontSize: 0` over canvas art and is not counted as text.

Listed palette pairs under 4.5:1 (muted or ash on cream, ash on ink, copper on cream, gold on cream, leaf on cream) are not used for the text this walk measured. Body text on cream uses ink or wood.

Pixelify Sans is bundled at `public/fonts/` under the SIL Open Font License (`public/fonts/OFL-PixelifySans.txt`). `@font-face` uses `font-display: block`.

IntroScreens `hit()` sets `fontSize: 0` on a transparent absolute target over the canvas (role picker and intro continue). Those nodes are not text and are not violations.

Body, dialogue, news, and buttons use Pixelify Sans at 16px (secondary 14px). Window titles and screen headings use CCPixel5x7 at 20 or 30. Chips, the taskbar clock, and the season label use CCPixel3x5 at 10 or 20.
